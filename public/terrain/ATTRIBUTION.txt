Atlas Watch world elevation overview

Mapzen Terrain Tiles, accessed November 12, 2026 (UTC) through the Registry of Open Data on AWS.
The source for both land and bathymetry at zoom 2 is NOAA's ETOPO1 Global Relief Model.
ETOPO1: Amante, C. and B. W. Eakins, 2009, NOAA National Geophysical Data Center.
https://doi.org/10.7289/V5C8276M
https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/data-sources.md
https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md

Atlas Watch joins the 16 Terrarium zoom-2 PNG tiles into a 1024 by 1024 Web Mercator grid,
rounds elevations to whole metres, and draws ocean depths at sea level in the overview.
Geographic LODs 0 through 5 can use the grid offline. More detailed terrain uses the remote source.
