Atlas Watch tiles this historical composite for an offline globe overview. The main tiles use Web Mercator.
The north and south caps use geographic coordinates. Atlas Watch downsamples the overview to smaller levels
and corrects source edge artifacts when detected. The accompanying project source manifest records the
requests, checksums and independent location/detail checks.
