infrastructure.json — data centres; dams and hydropower plants

© OpenStreetMap contributors. Available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/  —  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

This file is a derived database and is distributed under ODbL 1.0 (share-alike applies to the database, not to the
application code). Dams and hydropower plants were extracted via Open Infrastructure Map (https://openinframap.org).

Built by scripts/build-infrastructure.mjs from the OpenStreetMap-derived extracts published in God's Eye View
(https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view, src/data/local_data/datacenters and dams, ODbL 1.0). Each feature
was reduced to its OSM identity, one point (the area-weighted centre of its mapped outline) and a few descriptive
tags; contact details, notes and all other tags were removed. Source checksums and counts are in the file's
"source" field. The upstream extraction date was not recorded by that project; the dam set is a sample of a few
hundred sites, not every dam OpenStreetMap maps.


cables.json — telecommunication cables under water and under ground

© OpenStreetMap contributors. Available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0 (links above).

This file is a derived database and is distributed under ODbL 1.0. Built by scripts/build-cables.mjs from one
Overpass API reply (https://overpass-api.de, OpenStreetMap data as of 2026-11-15T15:02:06Z; the query is in the
script). Each way was reduced to its OSM id, whether it runs under water or under ground, its name, operator and
medium, and its mapped vertices rounded to five decimals. Nothing was added or extended: it is the part of the
world's cables that OpenStreetMap maps, drawn as mapped.
