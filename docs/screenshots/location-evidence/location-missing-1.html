Coverage limitations

  • Weather alert intersection uses the alert polygon when NWS supplies one. Alerts without a polygon are matched using the NWS forecast, county, and fire-weather zones reported for the location. If zones are unknown, the alert is reported as not determinable rather than as not affecting the location.
  • Earthquake distance is the geodesic distance from the location to the reported epicenter. It is not an estimate of shaking, damage, or personal danger.
  • Earthquakes are limited to the selected USGS summary feed window and minimum magnitude.
  • Data reflects the last successful retrieval shown above and may be delayed, revised, or incomplete. Atlas Watch only observes records while it is running.
  • A point outside the NWS zone service has unknown overall NWS alert coverage. Supplied polygons are still tested and confirmed intersections are retained; a missing zone match is not evidence of no alerts.
